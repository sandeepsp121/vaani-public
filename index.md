# VAANI (वाणी)

**Pro Recorder & Editor for Android.** Record your voice, clean it with AI that runs on your phone, edit it, mix several tracks and save it. Your recordings stay on your phone.

- [Privacy policy](privacy) · [गोपनीयता नीति (हिन्दी)](privacy-hi)
- [Third-party notices and licences](notices)
- **Support and bug reports:** [open an issue](https://github.com/sandeepsp121/vaani-public/issues)

VAANI has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. AI features run on the phone; the app only downloads an AI model file when you ask for one.

The source code of VAANI is private. This page only holds what a user or Google Play needs to read: the privacy policy, the notices of the parts VAANI uses, and the place to ask for help.
